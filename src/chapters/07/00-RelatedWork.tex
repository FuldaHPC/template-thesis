Remember that this is a scientific thesis.
A thorough literature review is therefore expected.
Identify relevant publications, established approaches, comparable systems, and previous research concerning the problem addressed by the thesis.

The chapter should address questions such as:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Which relevant approaches and findings already exist?
  \item How can they be grouped into categories or lines of research?
  \item What are the strengths and weaknesses of each approach?
  \item Which research gap or open problem remains?
  \item  How does this thesis address that gap?
\end{itemize}

Do not merely list or summarize individual publications one after another.
Compare and categorize them instead, and make the comparison explicit, for example in a table with one row per approach and one column per criterion.
All statements concerning previous research must be supported by appropriate scientific references.


Suitable tools for conducting the literature search include:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Google Scholar:~\url{https://scholar.google.com}
\end{itemize}
