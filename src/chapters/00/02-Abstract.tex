The abstract summarizes the entire thesis in a few sentences or paragraphs.
By reading it, the reader should be able to gain a general understanding of the work.

It should briefly answer the following questions:
\begin{itemize}
    \item What is the thesis about?
    \item What are the open research questions addressed?
    \item What are the main ideas and contributions?
    \item What are the main results?
\end{itemize}

The results should preferably be stated qualitatively.

Use plain text where possible.
Avoid formulas and references.

Although the abstract appears at the beginning of the thesis, it is usually written last.

As a general guideline, the abstract should be approximately half a page long and must not exceed one page.
